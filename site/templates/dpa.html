How the lens works

What the agent knows about {{ org.name }}, and where people stay in charge.

  1. Reads every story with {{ org.name }}'s profile. A public description of what {{ org.name }} does: {{ lens.profile.capabilities|length }} capabilities, {{ lens.profile.roles|length }} roles. Never client names or client data.
  2. Makes a call on each one. How much it matters (0 to 3), which capability it touches, a plain "so what", a move (try it, evaluate, watch, caution) and the first test to run. Products it names must appear in the story itself.
  3. Keeps the radar. Tools, models, techniques and rules worth placing go on Adopt, Trial, Assess or Hold, with the evidence and a logged reason for every move. Only a person can put something on Adopt.
  4. Turns Trial items into pilot proposals with a two-week plan, a success metric and data-safety rules, for an AI Champion to approve or reject.
  5. Tests the models itself. The task bench runs every free model the agent can reach on synthetic {{ org.name }}-style documents and scores them against known answers.
  6. Checks its own facts. A second model audits a sample of stories against their sources every day, and the results are published.
