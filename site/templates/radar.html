How the top {{ rv.top|length or 10 }} are picked

  1. Every story is assessed for {{ org.name }}. When it is about a specific model, tool, technique or rule that matters to our work, that item becomes a candidate and the agent places it on a ring, logging a reason for every move. It is tracking {{ rv.tracked }} right now.
  2. Only the {{ rv.top|length or 10 }} that matter most are shown, ranked by how relevant they are to {{ org.name }}, how big the development is, how widely it is covered, whether it calls for action now (try it, or pause), and how recent the evidence is. No more than four come from one quadrant. The rest stay tracked and can rise as evidence grows.
  3. Adopt is reserved for people. When the agent thinks something is ready, it stays on Trial marked "proposed for Adopt" until a person approves it. People can also override any ring.
  4. Models are tested on the task bench. The radar, including the full watchlist, is also available to other AI agents through our MCP server and JSON API.
