How the radar is kept

  1. Every story is assessed for {{ org.name }}. When it is about a specific model, tool, technique or rule that matters to our work (relevance 2 or 3), that item becomes a radar candidate. Names must appear in the story itself.
  2. Every three hours the agent reviews candidates with new evidence: new items get a ring; existing items move only when the new evidence clearly justifies it, and the reason is logged.
  3. Adopt is reserved for people. When the agent thinks something is ready, it stays on Trial marked "proposed for Adopt" until a person approves it. People can also override any ring.
  4. Trial items get a pilot proposal; models are tested on the task bench. The radar is also available to other AI agents through our MCP server and JSON API.
