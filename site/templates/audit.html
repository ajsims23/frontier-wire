How we're doing

Every day our newsroom agent checks itself against the wider press: what significant AI news did other outlets cover that we didn't, and why? Then it fixes what it can: it writes up stories it missed, adds outlets it wasn't watching, and learns from stories it underrated. This page shows all of it.
