The agents

Each is a step with its own job, tools and checks.

  1. GathererReads {{ st.sources }} sources every 3 hours, and the labs' own channels every 15 minutes for breaking news.
  2. Editor-in-chiefPlans each run: the most important threads, under-covered areas, and its own extra searches.
  3. EditorScores every item, merges coverage of the same event, drops non-AI news and duplicates.
  4. Research agentUses tools (news search, reading pages) to confirm stories that lack an official source.
  5. Writer and checkerWrites from the sources only. Quotes must match word for word; benchmark figures must appear in the source.
  6. Self-checkDaily: what did the wider press cover that we missed? Writes up misses, adds outlets, adds lessons to the editor's instructions.
  7. DPA lensAssesses every story for DPA, keeps the AI Radar and its top 10, writes what they mean for DPA and the weekly calls.
  8. Fact-checkerA second model audits a daily sample of stories claim by claim.
  9. Task benchTests every free model on DPA-style document tasks, weekly.
  10. Analyst and MCP serverAnswers questions from the archive with citations, and serves the archive, radar and bench to other AI agents. Connect yours

What a person decides

The agent proposes. These need a person.

  • Any change to the agent itself: sources, thresholds, prompts. The agent files a daily self-review with suggestions; nothing changes until a person approves it.
  • Moving anything into Adopt on the AI Radar, or overriding a ring.
  • Resolving anything the accuracy audit flags.
  • Sending the daily video recap to subscribers.
